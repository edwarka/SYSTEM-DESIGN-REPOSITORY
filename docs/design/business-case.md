# Business Case — Online Pillow Shopping Platform

## 1. Problem / Opportunity

Buying a pillow should be simple, but it can actually take a lot of time to find one that feels right. Customers often have to look through several stores or websites to compare pillows, prices, sizes, and materials. I see an opportunity to make this process easier by creating an online platform that puts different pillow options in one place.

## 2. Proposed Solution

I want to create an online store where customers can easily find and buy pillows that fit their needs and budget. The website would allow customers to compare prices and features, read reviews, and choose pillows based on things like firmness, material, size, and sleeping position. My goal is to make buying a pillow quick, convenient, and less confusing.

## 3. Options Considered

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------------|------|------|
| Option A | Sell pillows through an existing website or marketplace. | Easier and cheaper to get started. The marketplace already has customers who shop online. | I would have less control over how the products are presented and would have to pay marketplace fees. |
| Option B (recommended) | Create my own online pillow shopping website. | I would have more control over the shopping experience, branding, prices, and how I connect with customers. It also gives me more opportunities for long-term growth. | It would cost more to get started, and I would be responsible for running and maintaining the website. |

## 4. Feasibility

| Type | Assessment |
|------|------------|
| Operational — will people actually use/support this? | **Yes.** People already buy many household items online, so buying pillows online would not be unusual. I would focus on making the website simple and easy to use so customers can find what they need quickly. |
| Technical — can I build it with what I have/can get? | **Yes.** The basic technology needed for an online store is already available. I would need features such as product pages, search, online payments, inventory tracking, and order tracking. |
| Economic — does the payoff justify the cost? | **Yes.** I believe there is a good opportunity to make money through pillow sales and repeat customers. However, I would need to carefully manage advertising, inventory, shipping, and website costs. |
| Schedule — can it be done in a useful timeframe? | **Yes.** I could start with a basic version of the website instead of trying to build everything at once. This would allow me to launch sooner, test the idea, and make improvements based on customer feedback. |

## 5. Costs & Benefits

### Costs

| Item | One-time Cost | Ongoing Cost/Year |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Website and online store setup | \$5,000 - \$15,000 | \$1,200 - \$6,000 |
| Product pictures and descriptions | \$2,000 - \$5,000 | \$1,000 - \$3,000 |
| Starting inventory | \$10,000 - \$25,000 | Based on sales |
| Payment and shipping setup | \$2,000 - \$5,000 | \$2,000 - \$6,000 |
| Initial advertising and marketing | \$5,000 - \$15,000 | \$10,000 - \$30,000 |
| Website maintenance and customer support | \$2,000 - \$5,000 | \$10,000 - \$25,000 |

### Benefits

| Benefit | Tangible ($/time saved)? | Notes |
|---------|--------------------------|-------|
| More pillow sales | Direct revenue | Customers can shop whenever they want instead of being limited to store hours. |
| Saves customers time | Time saved | Customers can compare several pillows in one place instead of visiting multiple stores. |
| More customers | Revenue opportunity | I can reach customers outside of my local area through online sales. |
| Repeat customers | Direct revenue | Customers may return when they need a new pillow or other bedding products. |
| Easier decision-making | Indirect value | Reviews, filters, and product information can help customers choose the right pillow. |
| Better understanding of customers | Indirect value | I can use customer shopping information to understand which products are most popular and improve the business. |
| Build a recognizable brand | Intangible | Having my own website gives me the opportunity to build a brand that customers remember and trust. |

### Financial Estimate

For a rough financial estimate, I am assuming an initial investment of \$50,000 and an estimated annual benefit of \$60,000.

| Financial Measure | Estimate |
|-------------------|----------|
| Initial Investment | $50,000 |
| Estimated Annual Benefit | $60,000 |
| Estimated Net Return | $10,000 |
| Estimated Payback Period | 12–18 months |
| **Estimated First-Year ROI** | **20%** |

### ROI Calculation

I calculated the rough ROI using the following formula:

```text
ROI = (Benefit - Cost) / Cost × 100

ROI = ($60,000 - $50,000) / $50,000 × 100

ROI = $10,000 / $50,000 × 100

ROI = 20%
```


## 6. Priority & Urgency
This is a good opportunity because people are already comfortable shopping online for everyday products. Pillows are relatively easy to ship, and customers can benefit from being able to compare several choices without going from store to store.

Waiting too long could give other online pillow sellers more time to attract customers and build their reputation. Starting with a smaller version of the website would allow us to see if customers are interested before spending a large amount of money.

## 7. Recommendation
I recommend moving forward with Option B and starting an online pillow shopping platform, beginning with a smaller launch so we can test the idea, learn from customers, and grow the business over time.
